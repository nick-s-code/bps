About Us

Welcome to Brass Plumbing Solutions LLC, your trusted partner for all plumbing needs. With over 20 years of combined experience, our team of licensed professionals is dedicated to providing exceptional plumbing services to residential and commercial clients.

Founded in 2010, we've built our reputation on reliability, quality workmanship, and customer satisfaction. Our technicians are fully licensed, insured, and trained to handle everything from minor repairs to major installations.

At Brass Plumbing Solutions, we believe in transparency, fair pricing, and lasting solutions. We don't just fix the symptoms – we identify and resolve the root cause of your plumbing issues to prevent future problems.

Whether you're dealing with a leaky faucet, need a complete bathroom renovation, or require emergency plumbing services, our team is ready to exceed your expectations with prompt, professional service.
